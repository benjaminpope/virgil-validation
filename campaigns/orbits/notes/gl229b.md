Closure phases only (K band, 2.05-2.38 um). These are dual-field UT products with no standards in Phase 3, so V²
is not used, and no calibration template exists for them: the closure offsets use the loader's widths.

Two things about this system are already known and are not new problems. Single-night alias peaks (2024-02-27 and
2024-03-28 in earlier fits) are flagged by the pipeline: a flagged night seeds no position and its data still
count. The night 2024-12-18 disagreed in earlier fits; that is an open finding, reported and not tuned away.
