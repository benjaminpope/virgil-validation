The data are the provider's own calibrated OiDB files (their calibrators are not in them), binned by 8 channels
(`scripts/gravity_bin_calibrated.py`), with the virgil-vlti HIGH template laid out on them for gains and closure
offsets. The two 2021 split-polarisation nights are refused by the combined-polarisation template and left out.

There is resolved circumstellar emission in the field, which a two-point-source model does not describe. By the
ruling of 2026-10-07 this system is reported and never scored. A poor chi2 here is expected and is not a reason to
change the model.
