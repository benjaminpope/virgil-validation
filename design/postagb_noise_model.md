# Post-AGB imaging: does the virgil-vlti PIONIER noise model help?

*Status: measured on IW Car and on all nine targets (OzSTAR job `postagb_noise`, virgil-validation `f6d5e81`, virgil `46ce208`).
Tables are generated by `scripts/postagb_noise_check.py`; the fits are `scripts/postagb_imaging.py image / compare` with `--noise {current,vlti}`
and `--mean {blob,flat}`.*

## Question

The post-AGB GP fits (page `docs/imaging/postagb.md`) treat PIONIER V² and closure-phase errors as independent, rescaled by one MacKay
factor. virgil-vlti's pair test says otherwise: most of the V² scatter between consecutive exposures is a calibration gain per
(frame, telescope) and per (frame, baseline), shared by the six spectral channels, and the quoted errors are too large when treated as white.
`--noise vlti` applies that model: V² errors ×0.32, closure-phase errors ×0.65 (IW Car; each target has its own in the table below), gains on log|V|
per telescope (0.55 %) and baseline (0.45 %), a closure-phase offset per (frame, triangle) of 0.21°, and no MacKay rescaling (virgil refuses
`error_scale` with gains). `--mean flat` replaces the Gaussian-blob mean of the GP prior by a constant inside the support. Both are options; the
defaults are unchanged (`current`, `blob`).

Does the vlti model fix the fits (fewer grid-edge hyperparameters, residuals whiter, images closer to ORGANIC)? A 2 × 2 on IW Car
({current, vlti} × {blob, flat}) for mem, gp-iso, gp-polar (and gp-aniso on all targets), then all nine targets.

## Verdict

**Keep `current` / `blob` as the default and the published headline.** vlti/blob whitens the residuals as designed but does not improve the
images or the hyperparameter picks; vlti/flat is clearly worse, for a reason that has nothing to do with the noise model (below). The one
target where vlti changes the answer is PS Gem, where it is better: see below. The vlti options stay in the code as a sensitivity analysis,
reported here, and no published page number is refitted under vlti.

Judged against the criteria agreed for the decision:

| criterion | result (vlti/blob against current/blob) |
|---|---|
| (a) median NCC against ORGANIC, per-target deltas | medians unchanged (mem +0.00, gp-iso −0.02, gp-aniso 0.00, gp-polar 0.00). Per target mostly −0.04 to +0.02, but IW Car mem −0.24 and IW Car/IRAS 08544/AI Sco gp-iso −0.04 to −0.05; PS Gem +0.54 (mem), +0.68 (gp-iso), +0.13 (gp-aniso), +0.23 (gp-polar) |
| (b) grid-edge hyperparameters | gp-iso 2 of 8 against 3 of 8 on the same targets (4 of 9 with EN TrA, which is missing for vlti, see Caveats); gp-aniso 5 of 9 against 4 of 9; gp-polar 9 of 9 against 7 of 9. Not better |
| (c) residuals after subtracting the MAP gains | whitens: V² common-mode ratio 0.27 (white = 1; 3.55 for current/blob, which has no gains to subtract), adjacent-channel correlation −0.04 (0.64). The V² χ² under the fit's own covariance is still 1.67 (median), not 1: the 0.32 white factor is too small for imaging fits |
| (d) image differences in the strips | similar on IW Car for the GPs; mem under vlti is spiky on IW Car (cause not investigated); on PS Gem current/blob gives speckle and vlti/blob mem and gp-iso give a compact source like ORGANIC |

The rule for adopting vlti/blob was that it be no worse on (a), (b) and (d) and whiten (c). It whitens, but (b) is slightly worse and (a) and (d)
are mixed, with one large gain (PS Gem) and one large loss (IW Car mem), so the answer is the default stays.

**Why plain residuals rise under vlti.** The residual statistics in the main tables are on the *quoted* errors with the gains not subtracted
(the gains are marginalised, not fitted). Under vlti the image is no longer bent to absorb the gain pattern, so the V² common-mode ratio is
*higher* there (4.6 against 3.6, median over fits). That is the noise model working, not a failure. The "after MAP gains" columns subtract the
conditional gain estimate L Lᵀ C⁻¹ x per block and give the comparison that matters (`map_gain_stats` in `postagb_imaging.py`).

**vlti/flat.** The flat mean fails (median gp-iso NCC 0.71 against 0.89; gp-polar collapses onto the star; HR 4049 gp-iso drops to 0.11;
8 of 9 gp-iso fits sit on a grid edge), under current noise as well (AI Sco and HD 95767 gp-iso −0.27 and −0.47). So it says nothing about the noise model:
a flat mean puts no flux at the Gaussian blob where the start image has it, and the data cannot replace it. Reported separately in the tables.

**PS Gem.** Under current/blob every strategy returns speckle (NCC 0.06 to 0.20, χ²/pt 0.57, error scale 1.06) with a 5 to 6 % background at index 3;
under vlti/blob mem and gp-iso return a compact source at NCC 0.74, but with χ² (own covariance) 2.2 and, for mem, the background index at its
bound (6), which means the model fits poorly. ORGANIC's own PS Gem image is a compact source. This is the one target where the independent-error
model plausibly overfits noise; it deserves its own look before anyone cites PS Gem numbers from either model.

## Synthetic evidence check

To see whether the Laplace evidence can pick an interior hyperparameter cell at all: simulate IW Car's uv coverage from a smooth known disc
(a Gaussian ring with a first harmonic in the SPARCO scene, fluxes from the gp-iso fit), add noise in three ways (white at the quoted errors; independent
errors plus gains; gains drawn from the vlti model), and run the gp-iso grid.
All three cases choose the same cell: sigma 0.5, length 4.84 mas, the longest on the grid. The truth is smooth, so a long correlation length is the *right* answer; the
edge pick is a statement about this smooth truth and a long-length-limited grid, not evidence that noise correlations push fits to the edge. The grid
extends once at an edge pick in the real fits; synthetic grids were not extended. `design/postagb_noise/synth_*_s0.json`.

## Recommendation on refitting all nine targets

* Do not refit the published numbers under vlti: it does not improve the images or the picks, and its cross-noise evidence cannot be compared.
* The regenerated page is the current/blob rerun with the new pipeline defaults (table above).
* Look at PS Gem separately before drawing anything from it.
* A better case for a vlti-like model would need the white factor refitted for imaging (χ² under the fit's own covariance is 1.7 median, 1.8 to 2.6 on IW Car, not 1) and
  closure offsets subtracted in the residual analysis (not done here: the T3 columns are on the quoted errors only).
