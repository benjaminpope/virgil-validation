# Track B orbit targets: laptop smoke test

Generated by `scripts/trackb_smoke.py --nights 3` (2026-10-14, laptop, the virgil installed in `.venv`; no OzSTAR). It tests that the data are usable; it fits **no orbit** and uses **no published value as a start or bound**.

1. **Every file loads** alone with virgil under the Track B recipe (`scripts/trackb_fit.py`: GRAVITY science-channel closure phases outside Brγ; PIONIER V² and closure phases).
2. **Single-night blind binary fits** on three nights per system, spread over the paper's epochs: a grid search over (dRA, dDec, flux) from the data alone, then `fit`. *In range* means the separation is at most 1.5 a(1+e) + 1 mas of the published orbit (a projected separation cannot exceed the apastron distance). The paper's position is printed for reading only.

## Reading it
- The resolved systems (HD 210763, HD 70937, HD 224974 on its resolved night, NN Del, κ Vel) recover the paper's separation to a few hundredths of a mas on a night with no published value used. NN Del is already at raw χ²/N 0.8.
- Sub-resolution nights (paper separation below λ/2B) usually return a **wrong** peak: a large separation (6 to 29 mas) with a flux ratio of 2 to 3 per cent, which is what a blind grid does when the closure phases carry almost no binary signal. These are the nights on which the five Araucaria SB2s are *not* clean for Track B. They are expected failures, not faults of the data.
- Not explained by resolution: o Leo on 2022-03-30 (paper 4.3 mas, above λ/2B, fitted 14.3 mas at flux 0.036), HD 9312 on 2021-11-21 and HD 41255 on 2022-04-10 (a peak at 5.4 mas against 10.9, still inside the limit). Single-night blind fits find side-lobes; the orbit-level solver, which sees all nights, is what must reject them.
- κ Vel's first night has raw χ²/N 51: the single-calibrator, minimum-error issue the paper notes (bootstrap errors scaled by about 2.2).
- Raw χ²/N of 2 to 4 is typical for GRAVITY closure phases here; it is not tuned.
